### Task 1: Demolition Scope labor rows on the price sheet

The Demolition Scope block prints one labor line per family and action. The estimator fills in the hours. Today
`_fill` in `write.py` flags such a row PRICE PENDING, because the row has a quantity and the price book has no match.
The flag is wrong: the row is labor, not a priced device.

- A labor row with a known count shows the flag `HOURS BY ESTIMATOR` in the flag cell. It does not show PRICE
  PENDING. Its hours cell and its price cell stay empty. (Task 2 tests the red font of the flag cell.)
- A labor row with an unknown count shows QUANTITY PENDING, and only such a row does. The flag cell holds one flag,
  so that row shows QUANTITY PENDING. Today `_demolition_rows` never sets `quantity_pending`, so such a row has no
  flag.
- The Summary does not count a labor row as PRICE PENDING. The price check (`check.py`) does not count it either.
  Today it counts every row that has a quantity and no unit price.
- `_demolition_rows` in `rows.py` (about line 79) gives `_fill` no way to know that a row is a labor row. Add what
  you need to `Row`.
- A Demolition Scope line from `bms.render` always has a number (`DemolitionLine.count` is an int). Test the unknown
  count with an `Item` that you build by hand: `quantity=None`, text `Qty (##) AHU demo`.

Tests:
- Count 2: the flag cell says `HOURS BY ESTIMATOR`. The hours cell and the price cell are empty. PRICE PENDING does
  not appear on the row.
- Unknown count: the flag cell says QUANTITY PENDING. The price cell is empty.
- A device line with no price-book match is still PRICE PENDING. The Summary and the price check still count it.
- The Summary and the price check do not count a labor row.

## The estimator's ruling (binding)

Units shown on demolition plans are not counted in the new-work blocks. They print in a block headed
"Demolition Scope", the **last** block of the proposal, with lines in this form:

    Qty (##) AHU demo
    Qty (##) FCU Existing to Remain
    Qty (##) VAV Relocation

Named demolition work on a unit (status `demolish_with_work`) gets its own line with the work named, for example
`Qty (2) AHU demo, existing controls removed`. The Demolition Scope also goes on the price sheet as labor rows,
with the hours left for the estimator and the rows marked red. Every line cites the job (refs).

## Findings, ranked

**M1. A count drops silently in `serve_units` and `split_by_service`.** (must-fix)
`src/bms/plan.py:1381`, `:1384`, `:1415`, `:1422`. The moved and split parts are counted one per tag
(`Count(len(...))`). The rest goes through `_reduced`, which removes every number at or above the new value. A
family whose count is above its tag count loses the difference, with no `SetAside` and no question. Without a
register, the same ledger prints 6. The T-005b `split_pumps` has a guard for this (`plan.py:1283-1290`: count ≠ tags →
ask, do not split). The new functions do not. This breaks two rules: "keep the higher and add one question" and
"every dropped number on job.set_aside".
Fix: before a move or a split, if the family count is not the sum of the moving units' counts, do not split. Keep
the count and ask one question, or keep the higher and record the difference.

**M2. The split ignores `Unit.count`.** (must-fix)
`plan.py:1381`, `:1415`. A type tag, or a tag with a numeric quantity column (duty/standby), prints 1. This is below
the schedule floor that `check_answer` enforces (`takeoff.py:223-236`). Use `sum(u.count)` of the moving rows. The
same applies to an FCU-style type-tag family if S1 stays generic.

**M3. Four suite failures.** (must-fix)
- `tests/bms/test_contracts.py:459` pins `plan_job` to 4 parameters. This is lead-owned (hard rule 4): the lead must
  approve the new signature `register=None, typicals=()` and update the pin.
- `tests/bms/test_run_front_end.py:27`: the stub `plan(ledger, catalogue, dossier, playbook)` must accept `**kw`.
- HarkPro's report must not claim "zero failures" on `tests/bms` until both are fixed.

**S1. `split_by_service` splits any family, not only pumps.** (should-fix, or the lead rules)
`plan.py:1390-1427`. The task's decision is "split pumps by service". `Unit.with_plant` defaults to false
(`model.py:302`), so any family with two services splits, for example valves that serve no unit. Each split block
also copies all the sequence matches (`:1416`); `split_pumps` divides them by `_section_services`. `serve_units`
copies all matches into the target, even on a part move (`:1385`). These are two parallel paths next to T-005b's
`split_pumps` and `link_valves`. The T-005b review asked for `serves=` and `service_of=` on those two functions.
Recommend: feed the register into `split_pumps(service_of=...)` and `link_valves(serves=...)` instead.

**S2. The reason for a set-aside comes from today's code count, not from the dropped number.**
`takeoff.py:449`. `recount` takes the reason from the sheets that `placements` counts now. Since T-005a, that never
includes a demolition sheet. So the dropped 21, whose quote names "MD-401 5", is recorded only as "part plan of one
area". Render then prints "*AHU counted once on part plans" for it. `admit:505` gives any number in
(counted, explained] the first uncounted unit's status reason, wherever the number came from. The numbers stay
visible. Only the reason can be wrong.

**S3. Seam still open: T-005a's demolition title check overrides admitted pages.**
`inventory.py:767`. `is_demolition` skips a sheet that the register admits (`counts_units` true) when the code types
the sheet "Demolition" and its title names no new work. The recount is then low. The register line still enters the
max, so the printed count stays at or above the register's count. Bounded, but the register does not decide here.

**S4. Possible second count question per family.** (inferred, not seen on the fixture)
"How many X units?" does not match `_COUNT_QUESTION`, so `one_count_question_per_family` (`plan.py:1903`) does not
merge it with an older "Name (PREFIX): ...; confirm the count." question.

**S5. Task 2.6 deviation.** `render.py:940`. When sheets are named, the '*' line names the sheets but not the two
numbers. The task says "one short '*' line with both numbers". Lead to rule.

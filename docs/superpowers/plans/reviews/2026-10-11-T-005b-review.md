## Findings

### Should fix

1. **Ruling 18 (pumps in the plant block) is not done.** `split_pumps` (plan.py:1213-1295) always makes pump blocks
   of their own. It never asks whether a plant in scope (chiller, boiler) is the thing the pumps serve. It never writes
   which case it is. Probe (invented): CH-1, B-1 and P-1..3 with "P-1 chilled water pump serving CH-1" gave
   `P Chilled Water` with shape "Chilled Water System" beside a separate CH block. So the pump block takes the
   plant's shape while the plant is a block of its own: the plant lines can print twice. The 26-0544 case (remote
   pumps) is correct. Ruling 18 came on the afternoon of 2026-10-11, after the branch. The lead rules whether this
   is a follow-up or a blocker.
2. **A valve family's lower count is lost when the valves move.** plan.py:1368: `Count(len(tags), refs)` for a
   per-tag move has no `others` and asks no question. Probe: CV-21..24 (4) with a second count of 3 gave AHU "Control
   Valve" 4 with no other count and no question. The house rule says each other count is a '*' note with both
   numbers. `split_pumps` asks in the same case (plan.py:1281-1283); `link_valves` must do the same. A higher count
   is safe: the value is not one per tag, so the family moves whole with its count (probe: 6, others [4]).
3. **The prefix strip eats words.** `_tag_runs` (plan.py:1713-1718) is case-insensitive and matches the prefix plus
   any letters. For prefix "P", `_without_tags` removes "plumbing", "potable", "pump", "primary". The `_DOMESTIC`
   check in `_services_said` (plan.py:1704-1710) then does not see "plumbing". Probe: "P-3 plumbing hot water
   recirculation" went into the Hot Water Pump block (P-2, P-3; count 2). This is an over-count, not a lost line. The
   same strip with a valve prefix "V" removes "VAV-3" from a text, and the next unit word can then tie the valve to
   the wrong unit. Fix: a tag run must hold a digit (or the family's own tag forms).
4. **Demolition work goes to the unit's block, not to a Demolition Scope block (ruling 17).** `demolition_work`
   (plan.py:1408-1431) adds the removal fact to the first block it names (`named[0]`, plan.py:1426). Ruling 17 puts
   demolition in a "Demolition Scope" block, last in the proposal. This also answers the author's question 1 (c).
   When a fact names two blocks, only the first gets it, with no question. Keep the detection. Send the refs to the
   Demolition Scope block when the lead adds it.
5. **Job wording in a test.** `test_t005b_plan_blocks.py:289,295` uses "REMOVE OBSOLETE CONTROLS AT THE EXISTING
   TCMC" and asserts "TCMC". TCMC is the 26-0544 control-centre abbreviation. Tests use invented data only. Replace
   it with an invented name.

## Seams for the takeoff pass (unit register)

- **"Valve serves unit X" today:** `link_valves` (plan.py:1317-1391). The texts are the ledger facts of kinds link,
  location, device, note and provider, and the dossier's schedule rows (`_texts_naming`, plan.py:1191-1211). The tags
  come from a regex (`_named_tags`/`_tag_runs`, plan.py:1713-1739). The unit comes from `_served_by` → `_named`
  (plan.py:1398-1406, 1811-1822): by tag, else by key or prefix word. A valve tag that holds a unit tag (CV-AHU-1)
  also ties (plan.py:1350-1355). One unit ties. Two units for one tag make it torn, and it is asked.
- **"Pump service" today:** `split_pumps` (plan.py:1236-1245). The texts are the same, plus the sequence titles. The
  service comes from the `_SERVICES` regex (plan.py:94-102) through `_services_said`. A tag with two services is left
  over and asked. The sequence sections follow the service by title (`_section_services`, plan.py:1297-1315).
- **Can a caller supply the decisions?** Not as a parameter. A register can feed the branch today without code
  changes, as ledger facts: `link` (subject "CV-1", value "AHU-1") ties a valve. A fact that names a pump tag and one
  service gives the service. But such facts add evidence; they do not win. A regex text that disagrees makes the tag
  torn, and it is asked. To make the register win, add optional mappings: `link_valves(drafts, serves=None)` used in
  place of `targets` before plan.py:1358, and `split_pumps(drafts, service_of=None)` used in place of `said` at
  plan.py:1245. Each is about 10 lines and rewrites no logic. `plan_job` would need a keyword-only argument: the
  signature is in the interfaces spec, so the lead owns it.

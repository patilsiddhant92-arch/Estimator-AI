## The goal

The estimator revised `Brain/AME Std Pricing Spreadsheet.xlsx.xlsx`. It replaces the price book that the app uses
now (`assets/price_book/`). End devices must get prices on the price sheet.

## What to do

1. Find how the app reads prices today: the files in `assets/price_book/`, the code that loads them, and how a line
   on the price sheet finds its price (aliases). Write this in the report in 5 to 10 lines.
2. Make the app use the revised workbook. Choose the simplest way that keeps one source of truth: read the workbook
   directly, or convert it to the existing price-book format with a small script under `tools/` that the estimator can
   run again when the workbook changes. Say which you chose and why.
3. Fill `aliases.json` (or the file the app uses for aliases) so that the end devices the Playbook names
   (`playbook/shapes.json` slots and `playbook/wording.json` device words) find their price. List every device that
   still has no price.
4. Sheet 1 of the workbook lists information to look for in the specifications and drawings. Do not build that check
   now. Summarise in the report what it asks for, so the lead can plan it.
5. Tests with invented data only: a price is found for a device by its name and by an alias; a device with no price is
   reported, never priced at 0 without a word.

## The estimator's rulings (2026-10-11), the rules for this task

1. Demolition plans show the previous job's units (removed, existing to remain, relocated). A unit on a demolition
   plan is NOT counted, unless the documents name work on it (refurbishment of an existing unit, relocation of
   sensors or thermostats).
2. Part plans can share an area, so one unit can appear on two part plans: count it once; the other labels are
   duplicates (your T-004 answer 2: count by tag, never by occurrence, note the overlap in evidence).
3. "Quote #" is the job number (26-0544) and "Reference" is the project name, from the job folder name
   ("26-0544, Monmouth County Courthouse - Rplc of Air Handling & Fan Coil Units, NJ"), which is the parent of
   "2. Takeoffs". Never the takeoff folder's name, never "[project name]".
4. An existing BMS that is not Honeywell WEBS, Johnson FX (Facility Explorer) or Niagara Tridium prints in red with
   *** wherever its name appears (front-end heading, opening sentence), and the front end gets one flag line that AME
   works only with those three. The "[existing BMS name]" placeholder also prints red with ***. When the documents
   call for no front end, the proposal says "No front end information found".
5. A long clarification is presented as short points, not one long paragraph.
6. Never write "Assumed" when a schedule or the sequence states the fact: cite it as stated.
7. An item the documents mark FUTURE prints as its own line with "(FUTURE AS PER <source>)" in red font, as a flag,
   e.g. "Furnish and Install Duct-Mounted Supply Static Pressure Sensor (FUTURE AS PER control schematic on sheet
   M710.00)". Items the estimator must notice (e.g. an integral drain pan level sensor furnished by the factory) are
   highlighted red in the scope.
8. Control valves go under the unit they serve (AHU, FCU block), not a system of their own.
9. Pumps are their own blocks, split by service (chilled water, hot water).
10. Unit schedules (ERV and others) may list end devices shipped loose: read them into scope.
11. Review page: each question once.
Standing rules: the job's documents decide scope; every line cites this job; the highest count prints and every other
count is a '*' note with both numbers; a count that is too low is the worst error; the Playbook belongs to the
estimator (propose Playbook changes in your report, do not edit `playbook/`).

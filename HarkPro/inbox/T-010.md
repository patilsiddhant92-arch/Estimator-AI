  1. Ruling 23: ownership of the freezestat and the dirty-filter switch.
     - The rule, by case. The case comes from `System.control`: "field" is a unit with AME's DDC. "packaged" is a unit
       on factory controls. For any other value there is no check.
       - Unit with AME's DDC, for each of the two devices:
         - The line is "Furnish and Wire <device>": AME furnishes and wires it.
         - The line is "Wire <device> (Furnished by Unit Mfr.)" only when a passage that the line cites says the unit
           manufacturer furnishes the device, and that passage is in the sequence or in the controls diagram.
         - A schedule row or a schedule note is not enough. A note such as "furnished with the unit" does not move the
           device to the manufacturer.
         - Every other verb or owner is wrong. Example: "Furnish and Install", or "Install and Wire ... (Furnished by
           Unit Mfr.)" with no sequence or diagram passage behind it.
       - Packaged unit, freezestat only: a freezestat line is allowed only when a passage that the line cites is in
         the sequence or in the controls diagram and names a freezestat. Without that passage, there is no freezestat
         line. A filter switch inside a packaged unit stays under the rule "Nothing inside the unit is a line".
     - Which devices: a freezestat, and a dirty-filter switch. The documents also say filter switch, filter status
       and filter differential pressure switch. The Playbook shapes name them by the slots `freezestat` and
       `filter_dp` (`playbook/shapes.json`). Use the slot, the words of the device name, or both. Say which in the
       report, with the full list.
     - A packaged unit whose controller AME furnishes to the manufacturer (`playbook/rules.md`, "Unit control devices
       when AME provides the controls") is a case that `System.control` may not show. Say in the report how your check
       treats it.

         system:" and "Controls sheets that name it (control diagrams, points, sequences):". A schedule block does

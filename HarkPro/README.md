# HarkPro: work log for Estimator-AI

This folder is Hark's running record of work on this repo: bugs found, what is fixed, progress, and decisions from discussions with Siddhant. It is updated at regular intervals.

- `bugs.md`: bug list with status
- `progress.md`: dated progress log
- `discussion.md`: decisions made and open questions for the estimator
- `learnings.md`: what the estimator taught us and what the runs showed, in short general rules
- `sessions/`: one log per working day of the conversation with Siddhant
- `patches/fix-all-uncommitted.patch`: the current fix set (branch `hark/fix-all`, based on `7e27873`, from the `hark/evidence-ui` branch). **Not merged into the app code.** It is under review.

Per AGENTS.md rule 1, no real job names, tags, sheet numbers or job text are kept here. Test jobs are called Job 1 (one mechanical drawing), Job 2a/2b (two floors of one building, separate proposals) and Job 3 (large office fit-out), Job 4 (AHU and FCU replacement in a public building).
